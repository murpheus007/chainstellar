<h3 class="header-anchor-post">1.0 Background</h3><p>On January 21, 2026, popular streamer IShowSpeed arrived in Nigeria as part of his Africa tour. Amid massive fan crowds in Lagos, he celebrated his 21st birthday and visited a local jewelry store to purchase 18-karat diamond earrings.</p><p><span>When traditional payment methods like Apple Pay, Zelle, CashApp and Venmo proved unavailable,</span><span style="min-width:0;" data-state="closed"><sup>1</sup></span><span> he completed the transaction using USDC &amp; USDT. While this perfectly demonstrates real-world stablecoin utility, especially in a country where global payment rails are often inaccessible, it remains a highly uncommon occurrence.</span></p><p><span>Nigerians are deeply familiar with crypto.
